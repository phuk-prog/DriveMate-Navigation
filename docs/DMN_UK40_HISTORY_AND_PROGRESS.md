# Frozen UK40 diagnostic: old 18 cases and new directed-progress research

The previous 18 still-withheld UK40 case IDs are retained as an immutable
**historical audit cohort**, rather than silently replacing them with a new
subset when an opt-in research algorithm changes. Every one of the same 18
gets a new outcome (still withheld or resolved) and exact candidate geometry.
Default DMN remains verified at **17 correct, zero confidently wrong, 23 withheld**.
The new research option must not make confidently wrong source-generated matches,
fall below the previous opt-in 22 correct, or lose any source-correct old case.
This is not a change to the frozen original test oracle or road-law truth.
